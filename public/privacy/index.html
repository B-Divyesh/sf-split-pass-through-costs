Privacy

Plain-language policy · effective 28 August 2026

Your bills stay in your browser.

Slip details and attachments stay in this browser for this site. We do not receive, read, or sync them.

What leaves your device

The demo flow sends no external requests for supplier bill data.

Your control

Export a CSV, client line list, or JSON backup at any time. Backups omit attachments. Delete a slip to remove its attachment. Clearing site data removes local records.

Demo storage

The sample demo uses separate browser storage. Resetting or leaving the demo does not change your real slips.

Contact
