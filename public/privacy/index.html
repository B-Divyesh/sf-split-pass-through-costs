Privacy

Plain-language policy · effective 28 August 2026

Your bills stay in your browser.

Slip details and attachments are stored in browser storage for this site. We do not receive, read, or sync them.

What leaves your device

The free product makes no external requests for your bill data. It includes no advertising, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts.

Your control

Export a CSV, client text, or JSON backup at any time. Backups omit attachments. Delete a slip to remove its attachment. Clearing site data removes local records.

Demo storage

The sample demo uses a separate browser-storage namespace. Resetting or leaving the demo does not change your real slips.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project repository linked in the footer.
