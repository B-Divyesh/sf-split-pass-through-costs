Privacy

Plain-language policy · effective 28 August 2026

Control what leaves your browser.

Manual bill entry, storage, and exports stay in this browser. Split Cost Slip does not receive or sync that data.

What leaves your device

The demo sends no requests to other websites.

If you choose Extract bill details, the named attachment goes directly to the Sociobot gateway with your key.

You see the file name before sending. The gateway returns editable bill details. It never chooses billable or overhead.

Your Sociobot key stays in this browser until you remove it from the extraction panel.

Your control

When a split balances, export a CSV or client line list.

Export a JSON backup of saved slip details from the saved-slips panel. Backup files contain slip details, not attachment files.

Delete a slip to remove its attachment.

Demo storage

The sample demo uses separate browser storage. Resetting or leaving the demo does not change your real slips.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the project repository linked in the footer.
