S/ Split Cost SlipPrivacy

Plain-language policy · effective 28 August 2026

Your bills stay yours.

Split Cost Slip is local-first. Slip details and attachments are stored in your browser's IndexedDB on this device. We do not receive, read, or sync them.

What leaves your device

If you add a Pro license, the app sends only that license token to Sociobot's verification endpoint at api.sociobot.in, no more than once daily. Purchase checkout is hosted by Sociobot, with Dodo as merchant of record; their checkout policies apply to payment data. The app includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts.

Your control

You can export CSV, client text, and a JSON backup at any time. Backup files intentionally omit attachments. Delete a slip to remove that slip and its attachment from this browser. Clearing site data removes everything local, so export a backup first if you need it.

Permissions and retention

The app asks for a file only when you choose “Attach bill.” It does not use your camera, contacts, location, or bank account. Local records remain until you delete them or clear browser storage.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the repository linked from the app footer.
